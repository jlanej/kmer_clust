The thread through word-space prototype

Every chromosome is a walk: bin after bin, its vocabulary visits places on the map. Here the genome runs left→right and the map plane rotates around it — euchromatic stretches hum inside the mainland, centromeres dive to their satellite islands, acrocentric p-arms braid. Drag to rotate, ctrl-scroll to zoom, double-click to reset. It inherits the color mode, the selection, and the k slider above — sweep k and the threads reorganize.
