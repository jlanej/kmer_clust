Assembly compass prototype

Draft-assembly triage from one scan, no alignment: every contig is painted onto the reference at the span its vocabulary walks, colored by orientation (blue forward, red reverse — contig strand is arbitrary, so ~half of each is expected — lighter shades mostly ordered, grey scrambled or too short/unplaced to call), opacity by median exact Jaccard (J). Cover = the fraction of a contig's words this reference has ever seen; jumps = adjacent placed windows landing on different chromosomes or >5 Mb apart (order breaks — or an honest hop across an unplaced stretch). Lavender terrain marks satellite reference; ▼ flags a low-coverage (novel) run; ▪ marks a contig end that lands in satellite. Hover a contig for its full triage row; click to light its reference span up in the map, territory, and thread above. Reproduce for any assembly: python -m kmer_clust.project triage <assembly.fa.gz> "<label>".
