Projection prototype

Any assembly can be sketched with the same lottery, matched against the frozen vocabulary, and dropped onto this map — no aligner. Below: 4 Mb slices of the hard chr21 acrocentric region from two HPRC samples (plus a CHM13 self-slice as control). Each window travels: its own assembly coordinates → its place in word-space → its loci on T2T, where every dot floats above the genome line and drops a solid stem to its best locus and thinner stems to runner-ups — one origin, all its homes. Click a window to pin it: its full journey draws through every station at once, and the fine placement stage zooms into its primary locus to land it at 100 kb resolution by exact-Jaccard weight. Hover for hits and vocabulary coverage; a pinned window's loci light up in the views above.
