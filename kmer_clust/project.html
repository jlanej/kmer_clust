Projection prototype

Any assembly can be sketched with the same lottery, matched against the frozen vocabulary, and dropped into this map — no aligner. Below: 4 Mb slices of the hard chr21 acrocentric region from two HPRC samples (plus a CHM13 self-slice as control). Watch each window travel: its own assembly coordinates → its place in word-space → its best loci on T2T, with runner-up loci shown as echoes when the vocabulary genuinely belongs to several places. Hover a dot for its hits and how much of its vocabulary CHM13 even knows; click to light its loci up in the views above.
